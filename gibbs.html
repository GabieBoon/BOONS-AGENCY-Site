Biography

Haarlem-born and now Utrecht-based, GIBBS is a DJ who lives and breathes energy. Known for his unique blend of Hardhouse, Trance and UKG, he builds sets that climb from the first record to the last.

Promoters keep asking him back: four times at Club3 Haarlem, three at Hoognodig, twice at Nooduitgang, where he went on to play the mainstage. He has closed the night at TrainMore after Mr Belt & Wezol, at Levenslang Amsterdam, at Hoognodig and at Together Events, and played Patronaat, Podium Victorie, Veerplas Festival and Zomerpop.

In December 2025 he curated his own night at Club3, inviting Kellar, Tornado Kelly and Kay Ettema.

His sets are an unforgettable journey, driven by his passion and love for the music. Get ready for that GIBBS ENERGY.

Technical & hospitality rider

Kept deliberately short, and none of it is a dealbreaker. If your booth runs something else, just say so and we'll make it work.

Technical

Players (minimum 2, ideally 3 or 4)

  • Pioneer CDJ-3000 (X)
  • Pioneer CDJ-2000NXS2
  • Pioneer XDJ-XZ
  • Pioneer XDJ-RX3

Mixers, in order of preference

  1. Pioneer DJM-V10
  2. Allen & Heath Xone:96
  3. Pioneer DJM-A9

Preferred setup

DJM-V10 or Xone:96, with 3 or 4× CDJ-3000.

General

  • All gear set up, tested and operational before arrival
  • A technician present during soundcheck and performance
  • A sturdy, well-lit booth, free of vibration
  • A digital stage clock, visible from the booth

Hospitality

Drinks

  • 4 × Club-Mate original
  • 1 × bottle of vodka (Absolut or Grey Goose)
  • Still water, chilled where possible

Backstage

  • Clean towels for on-stage use
  • A small, quiet fan above 25 °C
  • A disposable camera
  • Somewhere to store a travel bag

Crew & guests

  • At least 4 guest list places for crew and personal guests
  • Access level agreed in advance, appropriate to the event

Travel

Based in Utrecht, travelling nationwide. Travel arrangements to be agreed per booking.

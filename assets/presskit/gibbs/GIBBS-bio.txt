GIBBS - Press kit
Bringing that GIBBS energy
Hardhouse / Trance / UKG - Haarlem-born, Utrecht-based, NL

FULL BIO
Haarlem-born and now Utrecht-based, GIBBS is a DJ who lives and breathes energy. Known for his unique blend of Hardhouse, Trance and UKG, he builds sets that climb from the first record to the last.

Promoters keep asking him back: four times at Club3 Haarlem, three at Hoognodig, twice at Nooduitgang, where he went on to play the mainstage. He has closed the night at TrainMore after Mr Belt & Wezol, at Levenslang Amsterdam, at Hoognodig and at Together Events, and played Patronaat, Podium Victorie, Veerplas Festival and Zomerpop.

In December 2025 he curated his own night at Club3, inviting Kellar, Tornado Kelly and Kay Ettema.

His sets are an unforgettable journey, driven by his passion and love for the music. Get ready for that GIBBS ENERGY.

About BOONS

High energy, from the first record to the last.

BOONS AGENCY represents and manages high-energy DJs. Every artist gets our full attention, and every promoter gets a straight answer.

Our story

Rooted in the Haarlem scene.

BOONS is a booking and artist management agency from Haarlem for high-energy DJs. We represent artists who bring energy to every slot. Opening a night is a craft of its own: building a room without burning it, so the floor is ready when the peak comes. Our artists know how to do both, from the first set of the night to the last.

We're selective about who we represent, and every artist on our roster gets real attention, not a place on a long list. Promoters deal with us directly: one point of contact, clear agreements and no waiting a week for a reply.

Our roster is growing. Are you a DJ who brings that energy? We'd like to hear from you.

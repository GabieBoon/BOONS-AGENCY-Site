About BOONS

A short list,
from Haarlem.

BOONS AGENCY is a booking and artist management agency for Hardhouse, Trance and UKG. We represent a small roster on purpose, so every artist gets our full attention and every promoter gets a straight answer.

Our story

Built in the clubs we book.

BOONS grew out of the Haarlem scene: the clubs, student nights and festivals where GIBBS and BURNEY learned to read a room. As their calendars filled up, from Club3 and Patronaat to the Nooduitgang mainstage, Zomerpop and Veerplas, the bookings needed someone to look after them properly.

That is what BOONS is for. We keep the roster short, we sign artists we can go all in for, and we deal with promoters directly. No layers of management, no waiting a week for a reply.

From request to showtime

  1. Step 1

    Request

    Send the date and a few details through the booking form or by email.

  2. Step 2

    Offer

    Within two working days you get availability and a price. We can hold the date for you for 7 days.

  3. Step 3

    Confirmation

    You confirm our booking confirmation in writing and the date is yours. Clear terms, no surprises.

  4. Step 4

    Showtime

    Rider, set time and travel are sorted in advance, and Finn is your contact on the night.

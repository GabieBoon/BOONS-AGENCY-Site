Biography

At just twenty years old, BURNEY is carving out his own space within the electronic music scene. Based in Haarlem, his sound combines euphoric trance melodies with the raw energy of hardhouse, heavy basslines and hardgroove influences.

Known for high-energy sets and powerful club moments, he brings an unpredictable mix of emotion, energy and underground intensity to the dance floor.

Alongside a run of festival and club shows, many of them back-to-back with GIBBS and including the Nooduitgang mainstage, he continues to develop his own productions. His release Worlds of Space is out now as a free download.

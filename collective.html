BOONS COLLECTIVE

The scene, pulling each other up.

BOONS COLLECTIVE is a network of DJs and creatives from the scene. We share gigs, tips and contacts, and bring each other along to bookings. Everyone can sign up: we look at every sign-up ourselves and bring in the people who fit.

Join the collective ↓

How it works

A network, not a label.

01

Support

Feedback on a mix or an edit, advice on a gig, a heads-up on a night that fits you. Questions go round the group.

02

Pull each other along

Can't make a date? Recommend someone from the collective. Need a DJ, visuals or someone to shoot your night? Ask the group first.

03

Grow together

New contacts, new rooms and new collaborations: a DJ who needs visuals, a videographer who needs a night to film.

The collective is separate from BOONS AGENCY: it is a network, not management or a booking agency. Joining is free and doesn't mean the agency represents you. Looking for representation? Join the roster instead.
